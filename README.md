# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard

This project presents a comprehensive **Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard** built to track key performance indicators (KPIs), analyze trends, and support hospital administration in making informed, data-driven decisions. The dashboard includes three main views: **Monthly View**, **Consolidated View**, and **Patient Details Tab**.

---

## 📆 1. Monthly View (Example: January 2024)

This view highlights key metrics and trends for a specific month, allowing users to monitor short-term performance and activity.

### 🔹 Key Metrics:
- **No. of Patients:** 513
- **Average Waiting Time:** 36.3 minutes
- **Patient Satisfaction Score:** 4.96
- **No. of Patients Referred:** 214

### 📊 Visual Highlights:
- **Patient Admission Status:**
  - Admitted: 269 (52.44%)
  - Not Admitted: 244 (47.56%)
- **% of Patients Seen Within 30 Minutes:**
  - Within Target: 316 (61.6%)
  - Target Missed: 197 (38.4%)
- **Patient Demographics:**
  - Age Group Distribution
  - Gender Breakdown
  - Race Distribution
  - Department Referrals

### 🕒 Peak Activity Analysis:
- **Most Active Day:** Monday (95 patients)
- **Hourly Heatmap:** Displays peak and low activity by time slot and day

- View hourly patient distribution for each day of the week

---

## 📋 3. Patient Details Tab

Provides a comprehensive list of patient-level data for deeper analysis, auditing, or operational insights.

### 📑 Table Columns:
- **Patient ID**
- **Full Name**
- **Gender**
- **Age**
- **Race**
- **Sum of Patient Wait Time**
- **Department Referral**
- **Patient Admission Status**

This granular view supports case-level tracking and trend identification.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- **BI Tool:** Power BI (based on layout & visuals)
- **Data Source:** Emergency Room Patient Logs
- **Visuals Used:** Bar Charts, Pie Charts, KPI Cards, Heatmaps, and Tables

- Real-time monitoring of emergency room efficiency
- Insight into patient demographics and referral trends
- Optimized staff allocation based on peak time analysis
- Actionable insights for hospital decision-makers
